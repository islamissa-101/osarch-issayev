# Price re-check: search-result prices vs live seller pages (15.11.2026)

My first draft used prices from search-engine snippets of e-katalog.kz pages. When I opened the
same pages live, several prices were stale, and two of them by a lot. The whole plan had to be
redesigned because of it. This table is the reason.

| Part | Snippet price | Live price (cheapest seller) | Change |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Kingston FURY Beast 2×16 GB DDR5-6000 | 230 168 (Flexcom) | 319 510 (Direct Technologies) | **+39 %** |
| RTX 5060 8 GB (Inno3D Twin X2) | 189 832 (Flexcom) | 314 106 (RS6, V2 model) | **+65 %** |
| ASUS BE27AQ | 116 074 (Flexcom) | 128 724 (Flexcom) | +11 % |
| Kingston NV3 1 TB | 70 931 (Flexcom) | 75 150 (Direct Technologies) | +6 % |
| MSI MAG A650BN | 22 940 (Forcecom) | 24 300 (Neom) | +6 % |
| Team Vulcan Z 2×8 GB DDR4 | 77 600 (Forcecom) | 72 080 (Forcecom) | −7 % |
| Intel i5-12400 OEM | 92 590 (min) | 76 290 (Ants) | −18 % |
| Windows 11 Pro DSP OEI KZ | 81 805 | 76 980 | −6 % |

**What it changed:**
- On the live prices, an AM5 + 32 GB DDR5 platform costs about **180 000 KZT more per machine**
  than LGA1700 + 32 GB DDR4. Ryzen 7 7700 OEM (91 726) + cooler (15 650) + B650M (72 911) +
  DDR5 (264 279) = 444 566. i5-14400 BOX with its own cooler (83 018) + B760 DDR4 (49 587) +
  DDR4 (131 460) = 264 065. The difference is 180 501. All four machines moved to
  one LGA1700/DDR4 platform.
- The designer's GPU went from ~190 000 to ~289 000 KZT. I kept it, because it is the one part
  this role cannot do without (see builds.md), but it is now the single biggest line in the budget.

**Lesson:** a price is only a price when you read it on the seller's page, with the date. The
brief's warning that memory and SSD prices move within a month turned out to be an understatement.
